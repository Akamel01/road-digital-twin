# Capture sources for Coquitlam, Surrey, Vancouver and Richmond

Issue #472 (part of #485). Researched 2026-11-24–25. Each claim carries the date its page was accessed.

The question is which sources other than an own rig can supply commercial-use data for Coquitlam, Surrey, Vancouver and Richmond for an in-service road safety review; the commercial licence is the gating fact. Method: desk research of primary sources; no vendor contacted, no quote requested. Not legal advice. “Fit for a paid pilot” means the licence supports a paid deliverable, coverage is adequate, and no rig is needed.

**Bottom line:** Open aerial/ortho data cover terrain in all four cities, including Coquitlam 2024/2025 imagery and Metro Vancouver Richmond-area ortho 2025, but no municipal street-level imagery was found on the pages searched. Surrey has point signs and polyline curbs under OGL – Surrey; Vancouver has wayfinding signage only; no curb or sign inventory was found on the pages searched for Coquitlam and Richmond. Bee's subsidised membership is US/Puerto Rico only and Bee LTE (Global) is separate, so a BC programme via Bee is unavailable; Hivemapper sells map data globally. Mapillary's terms address commercial use, but CC-BY-SA makes a closed paid deliverable conditional on attribution, share-alike and legal review. Panoramax depends on the instance; OSM-FR is unsuitable for a closed paid derivative without legal review. No service reviewed gives an unconditional commercial green light for a closed paid audit.

## 1. Municipal open data

No municipal mobile light detection and ranging (LiDAR) dataset or service was found for any of the four cities searched (accessed 2026-11-24): [Coquitlam](https://data.coquitlam.ca/), [Surrey](https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/online-services/open-data), [Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/) and [Richmond](https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm). Where a page does not state the acquisition method, the data is treated as aerial/terrain.

No City of Richmond open-data portal publishing geospatial data was found (accessed 2026-11-24): the [sale page](https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm), [Transparent Richmond report](https://richmondstandard.com/breaking-news/2020/02/20/city-launches-transparent-richmond-platform) and [public REST directory](https://corportal1.richmond.ca/server/rest/services). “Transparent Richmond” is financial/performance; the Interactive Map is a viewer. City geographic information system (GIS) layers are CAD 131.00 first / CAD 70.00 additional, price date not stated, licence unverified ([sale page](https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm), accessed 2026-11-24).

Open Government Licence – Metro Vancouver (OGL – Metro Vancouver), commercial + attribution ([licence](https://open-data-portal-metrovancouver.hub.arcgis.com/pages/Open%20Government%20Licence), accessed 2026-11-24). [Coquitlam River topobathymetric LiDAR 2023](https://data.coquitlam.ca/datasets/Coquitlam::coquitlam-river-topobathymetric-lidar) is LAS 1.4 river-bed survey data, not road data, under Open Government Licence – Coquitlam (OGL – Coquitlam) ([licence](https://coquitlam.ca/894/Open-Government-Licence), accessed 2026-11-24).

**Orthophotos** — The portal carries [Imagery 2024](https://data.coquitlam.ca/datasets/Coquitlam::city-of-coquitlam-imagery-2024) and [Imagery 2025](https://data.coquitlam.ca/datasets/Coquitlam::city-of-coquitlam-imagery-2025), both MrSID at 7.5 cm resolution and under OGL – Coquitlam, commercial + attribution ([licence](https://coquitlam.ca/894/Open-Government-Licence), accessed 2026-11-25). **Street-level imagery** — None found on the [city portal](https://data.coquitlam.ca/) (accessed 2026-11-24).

**Curb and sign inventories** — None found on the [city portal](https://data.coquitlam.ca/) (accessed 2026-11-24); the [roads layer](https://data.coquitlam.ca/datasets/Coquitlam::roads) is not an inventory.

## 2. Provincial and regional data

### LidarBC

The province announced CAD 38M for LidarBC ([news release](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023WLRS0054-001554), accessed 2026-11-25); the [programme page](https://lidar.gov.bc.ca/) states acquisition 2023–2027 and publication through 2029 (accessed 2026-11-25). ### TransLink

The [TransLink Open API](https://developer.translink.ca/) is an application programming interface (API) for General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) transit data only; registration and identification are required, and commercial use is under [TransLink's terms](https://developer.translink.ca/) (accessed 2026-11-24). Imagery or LiDAR not found (accessed 2026-11-24). Verdict: commercial under TransLink terms; not capture.

## 5. Street-imagery licences

### Google Street View

Google Maps End User Additional Terms were last modified 2026-01-27. It says users may not “use Google Maps to create or augment any other mapping-related dataset (including a mapping or navigation dataset, business listings database, mailing list, or telemarketing list) for use in a service that is a substitute for, or a substantially similar service to, Google Maps” ([terms](https://www.google.com/help/terms_maps), [policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/streetview/policies), accessed 2026-11-25). **Paid-audit verdict: Not allowed** — the clause blocks substitute-service datasets; the policies page also bars caching, storing and scraping ([policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/streetview/policies), accessed 2026-11-25).

### Mapillary

Mapillary Terms of Service are effective 2024-02-15. Section 12 states: “You may use the Mapillary Services only for the following commercial purposes: (i) improvement, training, and development of products, services, maps, studies, platforms, websites, applications, software, algorithms, datasets, solutions, or technologies; and (ii) in the provision of services for or on behalf of one or more of your clients.” ([terms](https://www.mapillary.com/legal/terms), accessed 2026-11-25). Imagery is CC-BY-SA 4.0 ([help](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409), accessed 2026-11-24). **Paid-audit verdict: Conditional** — attribution and share-alike require legal review for a closed paid deliverable.

### Bing Streetside

Microsoft Maps End User Terms were last updated 2021-11-01. Section 3(b) states: “You may not modify, copy, distribute, transmit, display, perform, reproduce, publish, license, create derivative works from, sublicense, transfer, assign, rent, sell, or otherwise convey any information, software, products, or services obtained from Bing Maps Services without the prior written approval from Microsoft.” ([terms](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/maps/bing-maps/product/enduserterms), accessed 2026-11-25). A Microsoft Q&A answer dated 2025-02-28 is supporting context only, not the licence ([Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/2344821), accessed 2026-11-24). **Paid-audit verdict: Conditional** — prior written approval is required.

### Cyclomedia

The General Terms dated 2025-03-01 state: “The licence is limited to the internal use of the Data and/or the Data Insights by the Client for the purposes set out in the Agreement.” Section 23.3 adds that “the Client must submit the intended purposes of use to Cyclomedia in writing for approval.” ([2025 terms](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69bd3518dd960ca00ae76dcc/69e0cf680bac25abc7141699_General_Terms_and_Conditions_Cyclomedia_Technology_B.V._20250301.pdf), accessed 2026-11-25). The 2021-01-01 terms used §24.2/§24.3 (unverified; page unreachable 2026-11-25). **Paid-audit verdict: Conditional** — internal use is the default and other purposes require written approval.

### Panoramax

Terms vary by instance. OSM-FR uses CC-BY-SA 4.0 for pictures; derived data may be under Licence Ouverte 2.0, CC-BY 4.0 or Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 ([OSM-FR terms](https://panoramax.openstreetmap.fr/terms-of-service), accessed 2026-11-25; JS-rendered; terms text not retrievable by fetch). The federation docs say the licence is meant to “avoid the creation of closed data, derivated database, artwork or picture” ([docs](https://docs.panoramax.fr/federated-catalog), accessed 2026-11-25). **Paid-audit verdict: Conditional** — instance terms apply; OSM-FR is not suitable for a closed paid derivative without legal review. Metro Vancouver coverage was not assessed.

These are licence readings, not legal advice; counsel should review before a paid launch.
